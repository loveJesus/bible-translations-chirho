## Particle Conventions

Use n-dash (–) for bound elements:
- Articles: "der–Himmel", "die–Erde"
- Prepositions: "im–Anfang", "auf–Antlitz"

## Grammar Notes

1. **Word Order**: V2 (verb second) in main clauses
   - SOV in subordinate clauses
   - Verb final in subordinates

2. **Case System**: German has 4 cases
   - Nominative: Subject
   - Accusative: Direct object
   - Dative: Indirect object
   - Genitive: Possession

3. **Gender**: Three genders (m/f/n)
   - der Herr (m.), die Gnade (f.), das Heil (n.)

4. **Capitalization**: All nouns capitalized
   - Gott, Herr, Glaube, Liebe

## Compound Words

German creates compounds freely:
- Glaubensbekenntnis (confession of faith)
- Barmherzigkeit (mercy)

Preserve word-by-word where possible, note compounds in decisions.

## Quality Standards

2. Maintain consistency with Luther Bible tradition
3. Follow current orthography (neue Rechtschreibung)
