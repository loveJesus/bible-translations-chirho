Standard Latin alphabet with Portuguese diacritics (á, â, ã, à, é, ê, í, ó, ô, õ, ú, ç).

## Particle Conventions

Use n-dash (–) for bound elements:
- Articles: "o–céu", "a–terra"
- Prepositions: "no–princípio", "sobre–face"

Portuguese contractions (no, na, do, da, ao, à) can be preserved.

## Grammar Notes

1. **Word Order**: SVO with flexibility
   - Similar to Spanish
   - Clitic pronouns can move

2. **Gender/Number**: Portuguese has m/f and singular/plural
   - Deus (m.), o Senhor (m.)
   - Agreement for adjectives and articles

3. **Personal Infinitive**: Unique to Portuguese
   - Allow infinitive to agree with subject

4. **Formal Register**: Use literary Portuguese
   - Maintain reverence for sacred text
