## Particle Conventions

Use n-dash (–) for bound elements:
- Articles: "le–ciel", "la–terre"
- Prepositions: "au–commencement", "sur–face"

French contractions (au, du, aux, des) can be preserved or expanded.

## Grammar Notes

1. **Word Order**: SVO with flexibility
   - Subject-verb inversion for questions
   - Adjectives often after noun (unlike English)

2. **Gender/Number**: French has m/f and singular/plural
   - Dieu (m.), le Seigneur (m.)
   - Agreement required for adjectives

3. **Articles**: Definite (le/la/les) and indefinite (un/une/des)
   - Partitive (du/de la) for mass nouns

4. **Formal Register**: Use literary French
   - Passé simple for narrative
   - Formal vocabulary

## Punctuation Notes

French has unique spacing rules:
- Space before : ; ! ?
- Guillemets: « text »
- No space after opening «, before closing »
