## Particle Conventions

Indonesian uses affixes and function words:
- dari (from)
- kepada (to)
- dalam (in)
- di atas (on/upon)

For bound Greek/Hebrew morphemes, use n-dash or natural Indonesian.

## Grammar Notes

1. **Word Order**: SVO
   - Relatively fixed

2. **Affixation**: Productive
   - me-/-kan (transitive verb)
   - ber- (intransitive/stative)
   - ke-/-an (noun/adjective)

3. **No Tense Marking**: Time words
   - sudah (already/past)
   - sedang (now/progressive)
   - akan (will/future)

4. **No Gender/Case**: Simple noun system
