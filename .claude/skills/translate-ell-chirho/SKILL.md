## Orthographic Conventions

Modern Greek uses monotonic system:
- Single acute accent (´): ά, έ, ή, ί, ό, ύ, ώ
- Diaeresis (¨) for separate vowels: ϊ, ϋ

Remove Koine diacritics:
- Breathings (ἀ, ἁ) → α
- Iota subscript (ᾳ) → α or αι
- Circumflex (ᾶ) → ά

## Grammar Notes

1. **Word Order**: SVO (more fixed than Koine)
   - Preserve emphasis where possible

2. **Verb System**: Simplified from Koine
   - No optative mood
   - Periphrastic futures (θα + verb)

3. **Case System**: Four cases (nominative, genitive, accusative, vocative)
   - Dative merged into genitive/accusative + preposition

4. **Articles**: Definite article same as Koine
   - ο, η, το (singular)
   - οι, οι, τα (plural)
