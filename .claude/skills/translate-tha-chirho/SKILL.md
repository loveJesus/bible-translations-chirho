## Thai Script Notes

- 44 consonants, 15 vowel symbols, 4 tone marks
- Vowels written before, after, above, or below consonants
- No spaces between words (spaces mark phrases/clauses)

## Grammar Notes

1. **Word Order**: SVO
   - Similar to English order

2. **Classifiers**: Essential with numbers
   - คน (person): สามคน (three people)
   - องค์ (sacred being): พระเจ้าองค์เดียว

3. **Tone System**: 5 tones
   - Mid, low, falling, high, rising
   - Inherent in spelling

4. **Royal/Sacred Register**: Important
   - พระ- prefix for sacred things
   - Special vocabulary for royalty/deity
