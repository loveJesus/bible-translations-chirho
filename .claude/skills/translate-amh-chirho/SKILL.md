## Ge'ez Script Notes

The script is syllabic with 7 vowel forms per consonant:
- 1st order: ä (default)
- 2nd order: u
- 3rd order: i
- 4th order: a
- 5th order: e
- 6th order: (schwa/none)
- 7th order: o

## Grammar Notes

1. **Word Order**: SOV
   - Verb final (Semitic pattern)

2. **Verb System**: Complex
   - Root + pattern system (like other Semitic)
   - Prefix and suffix conjugations

3. **Gender**: Two genders (m/f)
   - Verbs conjugate for gender

4. **Definite Article**: Suffix -u/-wa
   - ሰው (person) → ሰውዬ (the person)
