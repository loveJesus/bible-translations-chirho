# Yoruba (Yorùbá) Translation Skill

## Script: Latin

Latin alphabet with tonal diacritics and dot-below: ẹ, ọ, ṣ, and tone marks (à, á, etc.).

| Original | Yoruba | Notes |

| אלהים (Elohim) | Ọlọ́run | God |

## Tone System

Three tones (essential for meaning):
- High (´): á
- Mid (unmarked): a
- Low (`): à
