## Particle Conventions

Urdu uses postpositions. Represent bound Greek/Hebrew morphemes with n-dash:
- کا، کی، کے (possessive)
- میں (in)
- پر (on)
- کو (to/for)

## Grammar Notes

1. **Word Order**: SOV
   - Verb final
   - Similar to Hindi

2. **Formality**: Use respectful forms
   - آپ (aap) for deity references
   - Respectful verb conjugations

3. **Gender**: Two genders (m/f)
   - Match appropriately

4. **Vocabulary**: Prefer Perso-Arabic words over Sanskrit
   - خُدا (not ईश्वर)
   - مُحبّت (love) instead of प्यार
