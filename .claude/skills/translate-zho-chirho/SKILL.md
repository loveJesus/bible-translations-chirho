## Particle Conventions

Chinese particles are single characters. For bound Greek/Hebrew morphemes:
- Use n-dash: "那–天" (the–heaven)
- Or natural Chinese: "那天" (that sky/heaven)

Common particles:
- 的 (de) - possessive/attributive
- 了 (le) - completed action
- 吗 (ma) - question marker
- 着 (zhe) - ongoing action

## Grammar Notes

1. **Word Order**: SVO (similar to English)
   - Topic-prominent language
   - Time before place before manner

2. **No Inflection**: Chinese lacks conjugation
   - Tense from context or time words
   - No gender or number marking

3. **Classifiers**: Required with numbers
   - 一个人 (one person)
   - 一本书 (one book)

4. **No Articles**: No definite/indefinite articles
   - Use demonstratives (这, 那) if needed
