## Particle Conventions

Arabic particles are typically prefixed. Represent with hyphen or bound form:
- Definite article: الـ (al-)
- Conjunctions: وَ (wa-), فَ (fa-)
- Prepositions: بِ (bi-), لِ (li-), كَ (ka-)

## Grammar Notes

1. **Word Order**: VSO default in Arabic
   - Can preserve original order for emphasis
   - Adjust for natural Arabic flow

2. **Definiteness**: Use الـ appropriately
   - Divine names typically definite: الرَّبّ، اللّٰه

3. **Gender/Number**: Arabic has m/f/dual/plural
   - Preserve grammatical agreement

4. **Case Endings**: Use full vocalization for clarity
   - Nominative: ـُ
   - Accusative: ـَ
   - Genitive: ـِ

5. **Construct State (إضافة)**: For genitive relationships
   - "رسول المسيح" (messenger of Christ)

## Diacritical Marks

Include tashkīl (vowel marks) for clarity:
- فَتْحَة (fatḥa): ـَ
- ضَمَّة (ḍamma): ـُ
- كَسْرَة (kasra): ـِ
- سُكُون (sukūn): ـْ
- شَدَّة (shadda): ـّ

## Quality Standards

1. Use Classical/Standard Arabic (فُصحى)
2. Include full vocalization where ambiguity exists
3. Respect existing Arabic Bible traditions (Van Dyck, etc.)
