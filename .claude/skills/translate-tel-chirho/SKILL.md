## Particle Conventions

Telugu uses case suffixes:
- -యొక్క/-కి (genitive)
- -ను/-ని (accusative)
- -కు/-కి (dative)
- -లో (locative)

## Grammar Notes

1. **Word Order**: SOV (Dravidian)
   - Verb final
   - Agglutinative

2. **Gender**: Three genders
   - Masculine, feminine, neuter

3. **Honorifics**: Important
   - వారు (he-formal plural)

4. **Sandhi**: Sound changes at word boundaries
   - Apply Telugu sandhi rules
