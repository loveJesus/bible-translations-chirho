## Particle Conventions

Bengali postpositions:
- -র/-এর (possessive)
- -তে/-এ (locative)
- -কে (dative/accusative)

Use n-dash for bound Greek/Hebrew morphemes.

## Grammar Notes

1. **Word Order**: SOV
   - Verb final

2. **Case Markers**: Postpositional
   - Suffixed to nouns

3. **Verb System**: Complex conjugation
   - Tense, aspect, person markers

4. **Honorifics**: Three levels
   - তুই (intimate), তুমি (familiar), আপনি (formal)
   - Use আপনি for deity
