# Turkish (Türkçe) Translation Skill

## Script: Latin

Modern Turkish Latin alphabet with special characters: ç, ğ, ı, ö, ş, ü.

Note: ı (dotless i) and İ (dotted I) are distinct letters.

## Vowel Harmony

Turkish has front/back and rounded/unrounded vowel harmony:

**Back vowels**: a, ı, o, u
**Front vowels**: e, i, ö, ü

Suffixes must harmonize:
- ev-ler (houses) but kitap-lar (books)
- ev-de (in house) but okul-da (in school)

## Grammar Notes

1. **Word Order**: SOV
   - Verb final
   - Highly agglutinative

2. **Agglutination**: Extensive suffixation
   - ev-ler-imiz-de-ki-ler (those in our houses)

3. **No Gender**: Grammatically genderless
   - o = he/she/it

4. **Case System**: Six cases
   - Nominative, accusative, dative, locative, ablative, genitive
